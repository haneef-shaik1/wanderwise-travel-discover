# Credits & Image Licensing

## Important: the bundled images are illustrations, not photographs

WanderWise was built in a sandboxed environment whose network policy **blocked access to Unsplash, Pexels and Wikimedia Commons** (every download attempt was refused with HTTP 403 at the proxy). Because of that, **no third-party photographs are included in this project.**

Instead, all 14 images in `assets/images/` are **original illustrations generated procedurally with Python (Pillow + NumPy)**. They were created for this project, depict generic landscapes inspired by each destination, and are **not** photographs of the real places.

## Replacing the illustrations with real photographs

For a production or portfolio build, swap in real photography. Suggested sources:

- **Unsplash**: https://unsplash.com (Unsplash License: free to use, attribution appreciated)
- **Pexels**: https://www.pexels.com (Pexels License: free to use, attribution appreciated)
- **Wikimedia Commons**: https://commons.wikimedia.org (per-file licenses such as CC BY or CC BY-SA, so attribution is **required**)
